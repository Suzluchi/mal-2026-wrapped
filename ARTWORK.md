# Artwork notes

The loader uses the user-supplied Kid Goku walking/running sprite sheet. The filename credits krystaldragonx546; no ownership is claimed. The original file is in public/sprites/kid-goku-run.png. A display filter removes white; the source pixels are unchanged. Supplied GIF URLs are recorded in public/reactions/sources.json. PNG versions are first-frame stills for reduced motion and card exports.

Award cards use CSS symbols. Watching and reading energy cards display covers from the user's list. The older generated portrait atlas and character-image metadata are unused legacy assets, not displayed by the recap. Fictional demo covers are original SVG assets.

Music uses six user-supplied recordings, including audio extracted from the supplied Shinuka Ikiruka MP4. MUSIC-SELECTIONS.md lists the 40-second excerpts and their positions. The mix normalizes levels and crossfades between excerpts.

## Character-backed keepsake
The keepsake uses public MAL favourite-character portraits through the existing restricted image proxy. No generated mockup portrait or guessed favourite is shipped. Scenic backgrounds are original inline SVG city, ocean, and mountain shapes; they share the navy design and fall back to abstract scenery for unsupported characters. The generated chat mockups remain references only.

## Keepsake backgrounds and character art
The five background files in public/keepsake/backgrounds are supplied by the user. Autumn canal is the image-editor derivative previously shown to the user; the other four are copied without alteration. Original anime artwork remains the property of its respective creators.
Saitama artwork: https://onepunchman-anime.net/character/img/detail_saitama.png from the official character page https://onepunchman-anime.net/character/ . The layout clips one pose from the sheet using CSS and blends its lower edge. Character art is selected by favourite MAL character ID, never assigned to unrelated users. No character art is generated at runtime.
